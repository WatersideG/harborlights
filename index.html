About Harbor Lights

One waterfront property with an unusual combination of things to do.

A one-of-a-kind destination

Harbor Lights sits on Warwick Neck, at the mouth of Warwick Cove on Greenwich Bay. Slips for sail and power, nine holes of golf, an infinity pool, a post-and-beam restaurant, and event spaces under one roofline.

The reimagined clubhouse blends modern interiors with New England shingle-style architecture. The Grand Ballroom and the Back 9 host celebrations large and small.
